# Lantern Harbor (2-4 players, 45 minutes)

Players run rival fishing docks and compete to light the most lanterns along the harbor wall.

## Setup
Each player takes a dock board, 5 boat tokens, and 3 coins. Shuffle the 40 catch cards and deal 4 to each player. Place the lantern track in the middle of the table. The player who most recently ate fish goes first.

## Turn structure
On your turn you perform exactly two actions from this list: Sail (move a boat up to 3 tiles), Fish (draw 1 catch card from the boat's tile), Sell (trade a set of 3 matching catch cards for 4 coins), or Light (pay 5 coins to light the next lantern). You may repeat an action. Then you refill your hand to 4 catch cards.

## Storms
When the storm die shows a wave symbol at the end of a round, every boat that is more than 4 tiles from its dock is pushed back one tile toward the dock. Boats on a harbor tile are never affected by storms.

## Winning
The game ends immediately when a player lights the 8th lantern. That player wins. If the lantern track is not finished after the catch deck runs out twice, the player with the most lit lanterns wins; ties are broken by coins.
