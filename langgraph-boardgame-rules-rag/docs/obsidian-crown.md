# Obsidian Crown (3-5 players, 90 minutes)

A bluffing and area-control game in which noble houses compete to seize a cursed crown.

## Setup
Give each player a house screen, 12 influence cubes, and a secret loyalty card. Place the crown token on the throne space in the center of the map. Shuffle the 30 intrigue cards and give each player 2.

## Intrigue cards
You may play one intrigue card per turn, before moving. Cards marked "Reaction" can instead be played out of turn, in response to another player's move. A played intrigue card is discarded face up. The hand limit is 5 cards; discard down to 5 at the end of your turn.

## Claiming provinces
To claim a province, place influence cubes there equal to its strength number. If another house already holds the province, you must beat its cube count by at least 2. Defeated cubes return to their owner's supply.

## The crown
The player holding the crown token at the end of round 6 scores 5 bonus points. Whoever holds the crown may not take the Betray action. If the crown holder is eliminated from all provinces, the crown returns to the throne space.

## Winning
After round 6, each player scores 2 points per province plus bonuses. The highest score wins; on a tie, the player with the fewest intrigue cards in hand wins.
