# Moss & Mushroom (1-4 players, 30 minutes)

A cozy tile-laying game where players grow a forest floor of mushrooms and moss.

## Setup
Each player starts with one clearing tile and 6 spore tokens. Shuffle the 54 forest tiles into a face-down stack. Reveal 3 tiles to form the market.

## Placing tiles
On your turn, take one tile from the market and place it so at least one full edge touches your forest. Edges must match: moss meets moss, mushroom meets mushroom, and a stream edge may touch any edge. Then refill the market from the stack.

## Spores
When you place a tile that completes a ring of 6 mushrooms, gain 2 spore tokens. You may spend 1 spore token to swap a market tile for the top of the stack before taking your tile. Unspent spores are worth 1 point each at the end of the game.

## Solo mode
In a solo game, remove 2 market tiles at the end of each of your turns. You win if your final score reaches 40 points before the stack runs out.

## Winning
The game ends when the stack is empty. Score 1 point per tile in your largest connected mushroom group, plus spore points. Highest score wins.
