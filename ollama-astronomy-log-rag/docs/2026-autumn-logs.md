# Marlowe Ridge Astronomy Club - Autumn 2026 Observing Logs

## 2026-08-12 - Perseid meteor watch
Observer: Lena Vogt. Site: Marlowe Ridge lower field. Equipment: naked eye, reclining chairs, red flashlights, camera on tripod.
Seeing: not applicable, transparency good. Sky quality (SQM): 20.7 mag/arcsec^2. Temperature 14 C.
Targets: Perseid meteors. Lena counted 47 meteors in the first hour after midnight; two were bright fireballs with persistent trains.
Notes: moonrise at 01:30 washed out the sky for the last hour. Five members attended.

## 2026-09-26 - Saturn and the autumn planets
Observer: Tomas Brandt. Site: Marlowe Ridge lower field. Equipment: 8-inch Schmidt-Cassegrain, 9 mm eyepiece with 2x Barlow.
Seeing: 5/5, the steadiest of the year, transparency good. Sky quality (SQM): 20.8 mag/arcsec^2. Temperature 9 C.
Targets: Saturn showed the Cassini Division and four moons (Titan, Rhea, Tethys, Dione); Neptune was a tiny blue-grey disc; M31 Andromeda Galaxy core and M32 were visible despite the lower field's light dome.
Notes: collimated the SCT before the session; image was sharp at 300x.
