# Marlowe Ridge Astronomy Club - Loaner Equipment Roster

## Telescopes available to members
The club lends three instruments: a 6-inch f/5 Newtonian (kept by Tomas Brandt), an 8-inch Dobsonian (kept by Priya Anand), and a 10-inch Dobsonian (kept by Jonas Okafor). Loans last two weeks and must be booked at the monthly meeting.

## Observing sites
Marlowe Ridge lower field is the easiest site but has a light dome to the south. Marlowe Ridge upper pasture is 15 minutes further up the track and darker. Cedar Hollow is a 50-minute drive and is the club's darkest site; it requires a gate code from the secretary.
