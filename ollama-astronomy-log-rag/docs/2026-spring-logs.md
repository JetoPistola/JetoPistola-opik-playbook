# Marlowe Ridge Astronomy Club - Spring 2026 Observing Logs

## 2026-03-14 - Orion Nebula and winter clusters
Observer: Priya Anand. Site: Marlowe Ridge lower field. Equipment: 8-inch Dobsonian, 25 mm and 10 mm eyepieces, UHC filter.
Seeing: 3/5, transparency good. Sky quality (SQM): 20.9 mag/arcsec^2. Temperature 2 C.
Targets: M42 (Orion Nebula) showed the Trapezium clearly and nebulosity wings with the UHC filter; M41 and M35 open clusters were easy.
Notes: dew heater on the finder saved the night. Packed up at 23:40.

## 2026-04-04 - Galaxy season opener
Observer: Tomas Brandt. Site: Marlowe Ridge upper pasture. Equipment: 6-inch f/5 Newtonian on an equatorial mount, 32 mm eyepiece.
Seeing: 2/5 (turbulent), transparency fair. Sky quality (SQM): 21.2 mag/arcsec^2. Temperature 6 C.
Targets: M81 and M82 in Ursa Major were seen together in one field; M51 showed only its core, with no spiral arms visible.
Notes: wind gusts blurred planetary targets, so Jupiter was skipped.
