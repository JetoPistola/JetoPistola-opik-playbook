# Marlowe Ridge Astronomy Club - Summer 2026 Observing Logs

## 2026-06-20 - Globular clusters at the solstice
Observer: Priya Anand. Site: Marlowe Ridge upper pasture. Equipment: 8-inch Dobsonian, 12 mm eyepiece, no filter.
Seeing: 4/5, transparency fair, short night. Sky quality (SQM): 21.0 mag/arcsec^2. Temperature 17 C.
Targets: M13 in Hercules resolved into stars at the edges; M92 was smaller and more concentrated; M5 was bright but low in haze.
Notes: astronomical darkness lasted barely three hours.

## 2026-07-18 - Milky Way and Sagittarius
Observer: Jonas Okafor. Site: Cedar Hollow dark-sky site. Equipment: 10-inch Dobsonian, 20 mm and 9 mm eyepieces, OIII filter.
Seeing: 3/5, transparency excellent. Sky quality (SQM): 21.8 mag/arcsec^2, the darkest reading of the season. Temperature 19 C.
Targets: M8 (Lagoon Nebula) and M20 (Trifid) in the same sweep; M17 (Swan Nebula) showed a clear swan shape with the OIII filter; M22 globular cluster was spectacular.
Notes: Milky Way cast faint shadows after 01:00. First club night at Cedar Hollow.
