# Fornello Piccolo F-200 Home Espresso Machine — Owner's Manual

_Revision 4 — printed June 2025_

Thank you for buying the Fornello Piccolo F-200. Read this manual before first
use and keep it near the machine. Where this manual and the quick-start card
disagree, this manual is correct.

## 1. First use

Rinse the water tank and fill it to the MAX line with fresh, cold water. Place a
cup under the group head and run one empty shot of water before making coffee.
Never switch the machine on with an empty tank, because the pump can be damaged
within seconds of running dry.

The machine heats up in about 40 seconds. The green ring around the power button
glows steadily when the boiler is ready to brew.

## 2. Making espresso

Use 18 grams of finely ground coffee in the double basket. Tamp level with firm
pressure. The recommended brew time is 25 to 30 seconds for a 36 gram shot. The
pressure gauge should settle between 8 and 10 bar during extraction.

If the shot runs faster than 20 seconds the grind is too coarse. If it drips for
longer than 40 seconds the grind is too fine and you should loosen it by one
step.

## 3. Steaming milk

Turn the steam dial fully counter-clockwise and wait for the amber light to stop
blinking, which takes about 20 seconds. Purge the wand for two seconds before
and after every use. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth immediately after steaming,
because dried milk blocks the steam holes.

Do not leave the steam wand submerged in milk while the machine is heating. The
wand must never be pointed at anyone.

## 4. Cleaning and descaling

Backflush the group head with the blind basket once a week using one level
teaspoon of the supplied cleaning powder. Empty and rinse the drip tray daily.

Descale the machine every 3 months with the Fornello descaling solution only.
In areas with hard water (above 150 ppm) descale every 6 weeks instead. Do not
use vinegar or citric acid, as they damage the boiler gasket and void the
warranty. A descaling cycle takes about 25 minutes and the tank must be rinsed
twice afterwards.

## 5. Water and maintenance

Replace the charcoal water filter every 2 months or after 60 litres, whichever
comes first. The filter is not dishwasher safe. Change the group head gasket
once a year, or sooner if the portafilter no longer locks firmly.

## 6. Troubleshooting

No power: check that the plug is fully inserted and the rocker switch on the back
is set to I. If the machine still does not start, the thermal fuse may have
tripped; leave it unplugged for 30 minutes and try again.

Red blinking light: the tank is empty or not seated properly. Refill the tank
and push it firmly into the base until it clicks.

Weak or watery espresso: use a finer grind, increase the dose to 18 grams, and
check that the water filter is not older than 2 months.

Pump is loud: the tank is nearly empty or an air bubble is trapped. Run water
through the group head for 10 seconds with the portafilter removed.

## 7. Safety

The group head, steam wand, and drip tray surface reach temperatures above 90
degrees Celsius. Keep children away from the machine during use. Never immerse
the machine, its cord, or its plug in water.

## 8. Warranty

The F-200 carries a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The
warranty covers manufacturing defects in the pump, boiler, and electronics. It
does not cover damage caused by scale build-up, the use of non-approved
descaling agents, or normal wear of gaskets and filters. Keep your receipt:
claims cannot be processed without proof of purchase.
