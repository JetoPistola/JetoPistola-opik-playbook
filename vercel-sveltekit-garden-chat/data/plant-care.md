# Fernhollow Allotment: plant care notes

## Tomato (Sungold)
Water deeply twice a week rather than a little every day; irregular watering splits the fruit. Pinch out side shoots weekly. Feed with seaweed tomato feed every 10 days once the first truss has set. Yellow lower leaves late in summer are normal; yellow upper leaves usually mean a magnesium shortage (one spoon of Epsom salts in a watering can).

## Courgette
Hungry and thirsty: one bucket of water per plant per week. Pick fruit at 15 cm to keep the plant producing. White powder on the leaves is mildew; remove the worst leaves and water at the base only.

## Carrot (Nantes)
Needs stone-free, unmanured soil. Thin seedlings to 5 cm apart on a still evening, since the scent attracts carrot fly. A fleece or 60 cm high barrier keeps the fly out.

## Garlic
Plant cloves pointy end up, 5 cm deep, 15 cm apart. Stop watering when the leaves yellow in June. Lift when the bottom third of leaves has browned, then dry in a shed for three weeks.

## Slugs
Fernhollow uses nematode drench (applied on warm, damp evenings), beer traps, and crushed eggshell around seedlings only as a minor extra. No slug pellets are used at the allotment.

## Compost
Aim for roughly half green waste (kitchen scraps, grass) and half brown (cardboard, leaves). Turn monthly. Finished compost is dark, crumbly and smells of forest floor, usually after 8 to 12 months.
