# Fernhollow Allotment: planting calendar (cool-temperate zone, last frost ~ 20 April, first frost ~ 25 October)

| Crop | Start indoors | Plant out | Harvest window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomato (Sungold) | 1 March | 15 May | late July to October |
| Courgette | 15 April | 20 May | July to September |
| Broad bean | not needed | direct sow 10 March | June to July |
| Carrot (Nantes) | not needed | direct sow 1 April to 15 July | June to October |
| Garlic | not needed | direct sow 15 October | following July |
| Kale (Cavolo Nero) | 1 May | 10 June | October to March |
| Sweet pea | 1 February | 15 April | June to August |

Notes: a cold frame lets you sow carrots two weeks earlier. Never plant tender crops (tomato, courgette) before the 15th of May, even in a warm spell.
