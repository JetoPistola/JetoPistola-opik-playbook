# If you miss a dose (Larkspur Community Pharmacy leaflet L-02)

General rule: do not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten one. What to do depends on the medicine and on how late you are. The leaflet in your box always takes priority over this sheet.

- **Veldrine tablets:** taken only when needed for pain, so there is no schedule to catch up on. Leave at least 6 hours between tablets and never take more than 4 tablets in 24 hours.
- **Mirrowan syrup:** if you miss a dose, take it when you remember, then wait at least 4 hours before the next one.
- **Quillavet eye drops (twice daily):** if you miss a drop, use it when you remember unless the next one is due within 2 hours. In that case skip the missed one.
- **Tormisal inhaler (daily preventer):** if you miss a puff, take it when you remember, then go back to your usual time the next day.

This sheet is general information only. For anything about your own treatment, such as whether a late dose is safe for you or what to do after several missed doses, speak to the pharmacist or your doctor.
