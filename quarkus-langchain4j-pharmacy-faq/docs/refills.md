# Refills and repeat prescriptions (Larkspur Community Pharmacy leaflet L-04)

- **Ordering a refill:** ask at the counter, call 555-0142, or use the Larkspur app. Please order 3 working days before you run out.
- **Preparation time:** refills ordered before 14:00 are normally ready the next working day. Medicines that need to be ordered in, such as Quillavet eye drops, can take up to 3 working days.
- **Repeat prescriptions:** a repeat prescription is valid for 6 months or 6 issues, whichever comes first. After that you need a new prescription from your doctor.
- **Early refills:** we can release a refill up to 7 days early (for example before a trip). Anything earlier needs the pharmacist's approval.
- **Collection:** refills are held for 14 days, then returned to stock. Bring a photo ID if you are collecting for someone else.
- **Delivery:** free home delivery for customers over 65, within 5 km, on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Questions about changing a dose or switching medicines are for the pharmacist or your doctor, not something we can decide at the ordering desk.
