# Opening hours and services (Larkspur Community Pharmacy leaflet L-03)

Larkspur Community Pharmacy, 14 Tannery Row.

- Monday to Friday: 8:30 to 18:30
- Saturday: 9:00 to 17:00
- Sunday and public holidays: closed. The Harbourside late-night pharmacy (open until 22:00 daily) takes urgent prescriptions.

Services at the counter: prescription collection, blood pressure checks (no appointment needed, 5 minutes), inhaler technique reviews (book at the counter, 15 minutes) and flu vaccinations from October to January (booking needed).

A pharmacist is available for private questions at the consultation desk at all opening times except 13:00 to 13:45 on weekdays, when the pharmacist is on break and a technician covers the counter.
