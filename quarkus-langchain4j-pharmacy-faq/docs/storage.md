# Storing your medicines (Larkspur Community Pharmacy leaflet L-01)

Keep medicines in their original container, closed, in a cool dry place away from direct sunlight. A bedroom drawer or a high kitchen cupboard is usually better than a bathroom cabinet, where steam and warmth shorten shelf life.

- **Veldrine tablets (pain relief, 200 mg):** room temperature, below 25 C. Keep the foil strip in the box until you need a tablet.
- **Mirrowan syrup (cough, 100 ml):** store upright below 25 C. Once opened it must be used within 28 days; write the opening date on the label.
- **Quillavet eye drops:** keep unopened bottles in the fridge (2 to 8 C). After opening, keep at room temperature and discard after 30 days.
- **Tormisal inhaler:** keep at room temperature, never in a hot car. Do not puncture or burn the canister.

Always keep medicines out of the sight and reach of children. Return expired or unwanted medicines to the pharmacy counter for safe disposal; do not flush them or put them in household waste.
